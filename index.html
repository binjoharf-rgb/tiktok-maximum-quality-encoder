LOCAL-FIRST DESKTOP VIDEO TOOL

TikTok Maximum Quality Encoder

Prepare videos locally with FFmpeg while preserving the source resolution, frame rate, aspect ratio, color metadata, and audio whenever possible.

How it works

  1. The creator chooses a video stored on their computer.
  2. The desktop helper analyzes and encodes the file locally. Original files are never overwritten.
  3. With the creator’s explicit authorization, the app can upload a prepared MP4 as a TikTok draft using TikTok’s official Content Posting API.
  4. The creator reviews, edits, and posts the draft in TikTok.

The app does not ask for TikTok passwords or extract browser cookies. TikTok controls authorization through its official OAuth flow.
