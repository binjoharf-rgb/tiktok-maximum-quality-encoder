Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Local processing

TikTok Maximum Quality Encoder processes selected video files locally on the creator’s computer. The application does not send source videos to a third-party encoding service.

TikTok authorization and uploads

If the creator chooses TikTok upload, TikTok’s official OAuth flow handles authorization. The application requests only the permissions the creator approves. A prepared video is sent to TikTok only when the creator explicitly starts an upload.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect TikTok passwords, browser cookies, or TikTok authentication tokens outside TikTok’s official OAuth authorization flow. We do not sell personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app operator through the GitHub repository associated with this site.
